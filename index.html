Questions parents ask.

What are the school timings?

Classes run Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 2:00 pm. Nursery to Class II finish at 12:30 pm.

Which classes have admissions open?

Nursery to Class IX are open for 2027–28, with limited seats in the higher classes.

Is school transport available?

Yes. GPS-tracked buses cover the main areas of the city, with a conductor on every bus.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the class. Message us on WhatsApp or visit the office and we will share the fee structure.

Can I visit the school first?

Of course. Walk in during office hours or book a visit by WhatsApp.
